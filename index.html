03 · About the data

A few things to keep in mind

What is being counted

Every number is a count of crimes reported to the Chicago Police Department between 2022 and 2024, using the city’s public dataset. A “report” is one recorded incident, whatever its type or outcome.

What the numbers can’t tell you

Districts differ in size, population and how readily people report crime. A district with more reports is not necessarily more dangerous for any one person, and some crimes are never reported at all.
