03 · Method & sources

Read the map carefully

What a count means

Values represent records in the City of Chicago’s reported-crime dataset, aggregated by police district, month, hour and weekday, and primary offence type for 2022–2024. Forecast errors are taken from the accompanying report.

What it does not mean

The map does not adjust for district population, area, reporting behaviour, or exposure. Comparisons should be treated as incident counts rather than risk.
